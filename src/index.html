Help
  • Inclusion: the vehicle must stay inside. Large polygons are split into overlapping polygons; the area inside all of them is the original fence.
  • Exclusion: the vehicle must stay out. Large polygons are split into adjoining polygons that together cover the original area.
  • Edit a fence with : drag vertices, drag the small midpoints to add vertices, right-click (or Ctrl-click) a vertex to delete it.
  • .fence / .txt: in Mission Planner open the Plan screen, switch the dropdown from Mission to Fence, then Load File and Write.
  • fence.stg: with BRD_SD_FENCE set, ArduPilot keeps the fence in APM/fence.stg on the SD card. Copy the exported file there with the vehicle powered off.
  • Exported coordinates are always WGS-84, as ArduPilot expects, even in China mode.
